# 01 – Context

## The user and the team
- Team of 3 at the AI-Powered Business Challenge (UPB). The user is the **Tech Lead** and codes; treat them as an experienced engineer.
- The other two have little or no programming experience: plain language, one concrete example, one small task at a time.
- Roles used in the app (taken from the earlier handoff and the deliverables table; **names of the people are not known**):
  - **Tech Lead** – builds and publishes the web app, demo.
  - **Produs & UX** – promo material (poster/post), slide design, UX/UI.
  - **Business & QA** – strategy page, financial model, testing, pitch content.
- The user can set first names in the app (pencil icon in the sidebar).

## The contest (from the official rules and UPB page; see the Romanian handoff for sources)
- 3 hours on site. Theme, data, requirements and deadline are revealed at the start. Teams of 1–3. No programming experience needed. AI is allowed for everything. Mentors give feedback but do not make deliverables.
- **Six deliverables, all required:** (1) published web app with at least one testable main function, (2) one-page strategy, (3) financial model (editable table with formulas, one base scenario), (4) promo material, (5) AI log (max 1 page: tools, 3 prompts, 1 verified-and-corrected AI result, declared external resources), (6) pitch + demo (max 5 slides + link + test instructions).
- Preselection grid (rules take priority over the web page): App & UX 35, Strategy & financial model 25, Critical use of AI 15, Promo material 15, Delivered pitch 10. Top 10 teams with 60+ points go to the final (3 min pitch + demo, 2 min Q&A, 5 business jurors).
- Integrity: no fake features presented as real, no plagiarism, only enrolled members make deliverables, declare external resources in the AI log, use fictional data, no real personal/confidential data in AI tools.

## How to work with this team (from their brief)
Be direct, practical, critical. Think in MVP; cut auth, payments, real-time chat, admin panels, fragile integrations. Never invent APIs, results, statistics, users or rules. Separate known / assumed / to-verify. Do not claim something works if it was not run; say so. Small incremental changes, name the affected files, give verification steps. No generic AI course.
